# ABC Island 插圖角色

每張情境圖都用同一組角色，孩子才認得出「這是我」「這是我的朋友」。先用「角色定裝」把每個人各生一張（存到 `art/chars/`），之後生情境圖時把那張當參考圖。

| 代號 | 誰 | 外觀（生圖提示詞直接用英文這段） |
|---|---|---|
| `leo` | 主角「你」（男孩，5 歲） | a cheerful 5-year-old Taiwanese boy, short soft black hair with a little fringe, round face, rosy cheeks, yellow T-shirt, blue shorts, white sneakers |
| `mia` | 好朋友（女孩，5 歲） | a 5-year-old Taiwanese girl, black hair in two short pigtails with pink hair ties, green dress with a white collar, red shoes |
| `mom` | 媽媽 | a kind Taiwanese mother in her thirties, shoulder-length dark brown hair, soft orange cardigan over a cream top, long blue skirt |
| `dad` | 爸爸 | a friendly Taiwanese father in his thirties, short black hair, green polo shirt, beige trousers |
| `grandma` | 奶奶 | a smiling Taiwanese grandmother, short curly grey hair, round glasses, lavender blouse, dark trousers |

`SCENES.md` 列出每張圖的檔名、對應的英文句子和提示詞。
