# ABC Island 插圖角色

image-studio 從這份檔案讀角色（標題格式 `## 名字 \`代號\``）。角色定稿圖在 image-studio 專案的 `art/chars/<代號>.png`，生情境圖時當參考圖，每張圖裡的人才會長得一樣。

## Leo `leo`
The main character ("you" in the game): a cheerful 5-year-old Taiwanese boy, short soft black hair with a little fringe, round face, rosy cheeks, yellow T-shirt, blue shorts, white sneakers.

## Mia `mia`
Leo's best friend: a 5-year-old Taiwanese girl, black hair in two short pigtails with pink hair ties, green dress with a white collar, red shoes.

## Mom `mom`
A kind Taiwanese mother in her thirties, shoulder-length dark brown hair, soft orange cardigan over a cream top, long blue skirt.

## Dad `dad`
A friendly Taiwanese father in his thirties, short black hair, green polo shirt, beige trousers.

## Grandma `grandma`
A smiling Taiwanese grandmother, short curly grey hair, round glasses, lavender blouse, dark trousers.
