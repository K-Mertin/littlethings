# ABC Island 插圖角色

image-studio 從這份檔案讀角色（標題格式：`## 名字` 加上反引號包住的代號）。主角「你」以女生 Ella 為主、男生 Leo 為輔；朋友有女生 Mia 和男生 Ben。角色定稿圖在 image-studio 專案的 `art/chars/<代號>.png`，生情境圖時當參考圖。

## Leo `leo`
The second main character ("you" in some scenes): a cheerful 5-year-old Taiwanese boy, short soft black hair with a little fringe, round face, rosy cheeks, yellow T-shirt, blue shorts, white sneakers.

## Mia `mia`
A friend: a 5-year-old Taiwanese girl, black hair in two short pigtails with pink hair ties, green dress with a white collar, red shoes.

## Mom `mom`
A kind Taiwanese mother in her thirties, shoulder-length dark brown hair, soft orange cardigan over a cream top, long blue skirt.

## Dad `dad`
A friendly Taiwanese father in his thirties, short black hair, green polo shirt, beige trousers.

## Grandma `grandma`
A smiling Taiwanese grandmother, short curly grey hair, round glasses, lavender blouse, dark trousers.

## Ella `ella`
The main character ("you" in the game, used most often): a bright, cheerful 5-year-old Taiwanese girl, shoulder-length straight black bob with a straight fringe and a small yellow star hair clip, round face, rosy cheeks, pink T-shirt, denim pinafore skirt, white socks, pink sneakers.

## Ben `ben`
A friend: a playful 5-year-old Taiwanese boy, short spiky black hair, round red glasses, red-and-white striped T-shirt, khaki shorts, blue sneakers.
