# Letter sounds from Wikimedia Commons

Every phonics sound in ABC Island comes from one source: the IPA sound recordings on Wikimedia Commons
(the ones Wikipedia's phonetics articles use), licensed **CC BY-SA 3.0** (most files also GFDL).
The game uses short cuts of them; the cut clips are shared under the same license.
`../cut-wikimedia.py` makes the cuts; `meta.json` has each file's page, uploader and description.
The game shows these credits from the ⓘ button on the Phonics and Blends screens.
