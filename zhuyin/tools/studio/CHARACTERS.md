# ㄅㄆㄇ 小島 吉祥物

孩子可以在首頁 🐾 換吉祥物。每隻都有三張半身像：平常（idle）、講話張嘴（talk）、眨眼（blink），講話時嘴巴開合。

## 小藍（企鵝） `penguin`
Xiao Lan, a small cute baby penguin: deep cornflower-blue head and back, a big round white face patch and white belly, a small orange beak, big round dark shiny eyes, rosy pink cheeks, little blue flippers.

## 藍鵲 `magpie`
A cute young Taiwan blue magpie (Formosan blue magpie) chick: glossy black head, a small bright red beak, big round dark shiny eyes with a thin yellow ring, vivid cobalt-blue body and wings with a few white tips, rosy cheeks.

## 兔兔 `rabbit`
A cute little white bunny: soft fluffy white fur, long upright ears with pink insides, a small pink nose, big round dark shiny eyes, rosy pink cheeks.

## 狗狗 `dog`
A cute little puppy: warm light-brown fur, darker brown floppy ears, a cream muzzle, a small dark nose, big round dark shiny eyes, rosy cheeks.

## 貓貓 `cat`
A cute little orange tabby kitten: soft orange fur with darker orange stripes on the forehead, cream muzzle and chest, pink inside the ears, a small pink nose, a few whiskers, big round dark shiny eyes, rosy cheeks.
